EUROPE SUMMER TRIP — June 2024
=============================

Day 1 — June 3: Landed in Lisbon (LIS) around 10am. Walked Alfama, watched fado show in the evening.

Day 2 — June 4: Sintra day trip. Pena Palace all morning, Quinta da Regaleira in the afternoon. Back to Lisbon for dinner.

Day 3 — June 5: Morning flight to Madrid. Afternoon: Prado Museum and Retiro Park.

June 6: Madrid -> Toledo by train (30 min). Cathedral + Alcázar.

Day 5 — June 7: Flew to Barcelona. Sagrada Família booked for 3pm, then La Rambla at sunset.

June 8: Park Güell in the morning; Barceloneta beach afternoon. Tapas crawl in El Born at night.

June 9: Train to Girona. Old town, cathedral steps (Game of Thrones vibes). Overnight in Barcelona.

Day 8 — June 10: Flight back home. Trip complete!
